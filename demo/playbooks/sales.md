# Playbook: Sales (Outbound)

Use this context for outbound sales calls.

## Mindset
- You're not selling. You're helping them decide.
- Use what you know about them — that's your advantage.
- If they're not ready, don't push. Note it and follow up later.

## Before the Call
Review their `customer.md` and `memory.md`:
- What plan are they on?
- What have previous conversations revealed?
- Any pain points mentioned?
- Any expansion signals?

## Opening
Don't be cold. You have history.

❌ "Hi, this is Alex from Acme, do you have a moment?"
✅ "Hey Sarah, it's Alex from Acme. Last time we talked you mentioned your team was growing — wanted to check in on that."

## Discovery Questions
- "How's the team using Acme these days?"
- "Any workflows that feel clunky?"
- "What would make your life easier?"

## If Upgrade Makes Sense
- Connect their pain to the solution
- Be specific: "Team plan gives you X, which solves Y you mentioned"
- Offer trial of higher tier if hesitant

## If Not Ready
- Note in memory.md
- Ask when to follow up
- Leave value: "Happy to send over a comparison doc"

## Closing
- Clear next step (upgrade, trial, follow-up date)
- Update memory.md with outcome
- Schedule follow-up if applicable
