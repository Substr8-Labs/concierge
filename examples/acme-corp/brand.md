# Acme Corp — Brand Guidelines

## Company

- **Name:** Acme Corp
- **Industry:** E-commerce (general merchandise)
- **Founded:** 2015
- **HQ:** San Francisco, CA

## Voice & Tone

- **Professional but warm** — We're experts who care
- **Clear and concise** — Value customer's time
- **Solution-oriented** — Focus on fixing, not blaming
- **Empathetic** — Acknowledge frustration, then solve

## Policies

### Refunds
- Full refund within 30 days of purchase
- No questions asked under $100
- Refunds over $100 require manager approval
- Damaged items: immediate refund + replacement option

### Shipping
- Standard: 5-7 business days
- Express: 2-3 business days
- Same-day: Available in select cities
- Free shipping over $50

### Returns
- Easy 30-day returns
- Free return shipping for defects
- Customer pays return shipping for change-of-mind

### Support Hours
- 24/7 chat support
- Phone: 9am-9pm PST
- Email: < 24 hour response

## Common Issues & Resolutions

### Order Not Arrived
1. Check tracking information
2. If past delivery date: contact carrier
3. If carrier confirms lost: full refund or reship

### Wrong Item Received
1. Apologize
2. Ship correct item immediately
3. Provide prepaid return label for wrong item
4. Offer 10% discount on next order

### Damaged Item
1. Apologize
2. Offer: full refund OR replacement
3. No need to return damaged item (< $50 value)
4. Provide prepaid label for items > $50

## Language Guidelines

### Use
- "Happy to help"
- "Let me take care of that"
- "I understand"
- "Great question"

### Avoid
- "Unfortunately" (reframe positively)
- "Our policy states..." (sound human)
- "That's not my department"
- "You should have..."
